# Design quality

Adapted from [Impeccable's craft floor](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/8409a564d1baee90b2dde4f7a8f06288838e3921/.github/skills/impeccable/reference/craft-floor.md) and retained Anthropic visual guidance.

## Hierarchy and content

Make purpose, important state and the primary action findable. Group related content and separate different groups. Numbering should carry an actual sequence.

Use realistic content lengths. Keep action names consistent with feedback. Polishing does not authorize replacing factual content.

## Type and structure

Use existing typography deliberately: roles, scale, weight, measure and spacing. A product UI can work with one family; two need a purpose.

Borders, shadows and surfaces express structure/elevation. Cards group independent content, rather than every paragraph. Match radius and spacing to existing tokens.

Pinned palettes and aesthetics are requirements. Overused treatments invite an intent check, not an absolute ban.

## Complete behavior and evidence

Include states the task needs and preserve usable controls, clear labels, readable text and responsive composition. Do not add unrelated specialist reviews.

Source, type and DOM checks establish logic and markup contracts, not actual spacing, clipping or appearance on every device. Report that limit; do not claim an observed visual improvement from source inspection alone.
