## Catalog policy

1. `core` means eligible for routine loading; it does not mean every core skill is loaded on every task.
2. `on-demand` skills require direct task evidence (framework, library, workflow, or audit type).
3. Do not remove a skill while a workflow, manifest, rule, template, or `related_skills` entry still references it.
4. Upstream updates must be pinned, license-tracked, validated, and installed through the repository's `~/.copilot` layout before the old revision is removed.
5. Official or maintainer-owned skills are preferred for library-specific behavior; project-curated workflows remain authoritative for staged-only, branch-SHA, quorum, and commit-message constraints.
